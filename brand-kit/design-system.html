01 / Wordmark

The wordmark, locked.

Outfit — a contemporary geometric sans — with the keyword weighted. Automations is the search term and the editorial center of gravity, so the type puts it there: big and bold, with a single teal period. “The” and “Guide” frame it at small, quiet weight.

Production note Outfit is the evaluation typeface — it nails the geometric, confident shape. If the brand earns budget, the production cut should be a paid grotesque (Söhne, GT America, or Founders Grotesk) for sharper detailing at large sizes. The layout, weights, and teal period carry over unchanged.

Lockups

The same wordmark across the contexts the brand actually lives in — site header, centered email masthead, reversed on dark, and one-color for sponsor and merch use.

02 / Color

One brand color, two surfaces, three accents.

Teal stays. The hex moves slightly — from #2dd4bf (a candy teal) to #14a890 (a more authoritative, ink-friendly teal). Same family, more weight. The full ramp gives you AAA-contrast options on both light and dark backgrounds.

05 / Newsletter Templates

Two cadences. One masthead. Compounding trust.

Your reader sees the same wordmark and the same teal accent whether they get the daily or the weekly. Format changes; identity doesn’t. Each template is responsive 600px-wide, inbox-safe HTML.

Beehiiv-ready Import these straight into Beehiiv

The previews below are the design-fidelity versions (with Google Fonts loaded). For sending, use the inbox-safe versions in beehiiv/ — all CSS inlined, no <style> blocks (Beehiiv strips them), system-font fallbacks (Georgia for the serif), table-based layout, tested for Outlook/Gmail/Apple Mail.
