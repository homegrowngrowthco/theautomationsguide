# SEO Audit — theautomationsguide.com — 2026-07-07

Full-site audit (no URL scope). Production audited.

## Verdict

The site is in excellent technical SEO shape. Crawl infrastructure, redirects, headers, structured data, E-E-A-T signals, and Lighthouse SEO scores are all clean, and 122 of 129 sitemap URLs are indexed (94.6%). There are zero hard on-page issues. The only items worth attention are advisory: a blog-post mobile LCP of 2.6s sitting just over the 2.5s Core Web Vitals threshold, 108 titles over 62 chars, 51 tool-page titles containing em/en dashes, and 7 URLs not yet indexed (5 of which are brand-new pages where "unknown to Google" is expected).

## Prioritized fix list

1. **Blog-post mobile LCP (Medium):** identify the LCP element on long posts (likely hero image or webfont-blocked heading) and shave ~0.2s: preload the hero, check image sizing/format, confirm fonts are `font-display: swap`. Retest 2-3 posts.
2. **Request Indexing (Low, 5 min):** in GSC, request indexing for the 7 non-indexed URLs, prioritizing the two "Crawled - currently not indexed" posts plus today's AI-SDR post and its two tool pages (`/tools/11x/`, `/tools/artisan/`).
3. **Internal links (Low):** add contextual links from indexed, related posts to the two crawled-not-indexed posts.
4. **Tool-page title template (Low):** remove the em/en dash from the `/tools/` `<title>` template (fixes all 51 in one edit) and consider tightening the pattern to bring titles under 62 chars, which would also cut into the 108 long-title bucket.
