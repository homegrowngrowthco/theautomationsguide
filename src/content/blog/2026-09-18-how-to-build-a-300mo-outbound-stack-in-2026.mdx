## The Stack and Why These Four Tools

Before I walk through the build, here is the logic behind each choice. Every tool earns its seat by doing one job well and exposing a clean API so [n8n](/go/n8n/) can stitch everything together without you paying for native integrations.

The monthly math: [Apollo](/go/apollo/) Basic at $49/mo gives you 10,000 export credits, contact search, and basic sequencing you will not actually use (Smartlead is the better sender, full stop). [Smartlead](/go/smartlead/) Starter at $39/mo gives you unlimited email accounts, inbox rotation, and warmup built in. [Pipedrive](/go/pipedrive/) Essential at $14/seat/mo handles your deal pipeline and contact records. n8n Cloud at $20/mo covers the glue layer. Add a domain purchase for sending infrastructure and you land at roughly $292/mo.

The workflow covers four phases: pull and enrich leads from Apollo, push them into Smartlead sequences, log replies and meetings back to Pipedrive, and trigger follow-up tasks automatically when a deal goes cold.

## Pipedrive as the Source of Truth

The reason I use [Pipedrive](/tools/pipedrive/) instead of a free CRM tier is API quality and pipeline visibility. At $14/seat it is the most affordable CRM with a proper REST API, webhook support, and enough field customization to store everything n8n needs to route deals correctly. Free HubSpot locks API access behind paid plans. The free Salesforce Developer Edition is not a production CRM. Do not bother with either at this budget.

For this stack, create three custom fields on the Pipedrive Deal object: `smartlead_campaign_id` (text), `last_reply_sentiment` (single option: interested, not interested, oor), and `sequence_status` (single option: active, completed, suppressed). n8n writes to all three via the Deals API. This gives you one place to see every contact's outbound status without logging into Smartlead separately. If you ever need to [migrate from Pipedrive to a heavier CRM](/blog/2026-09-17-migrate-from-pipedrive-to-close-without-losing-your-sequence/) down the road, these structured fields make the transition clean.

<MyTake>
Teams at this budget almost always over-engineer the personalization layer and completely neglect the follow-up logic. A two-step cold sequence backed by solid follow-up automation beats a seven-step hyper-personalized sequence with zero CRM logging. Every time. The money is in the fifth touchpoint, not the first one.
</MyTake>

## Who This Stack Is and Is Not For

This build is designed for a single SDR or a founder running their own sales motion, targeting 300 to 800 new contacts per month. If you are pushing 5,000+ contacts per month, you will hit Apollo's export limits and Smartlead's Starter plan caps. At that volume, you also want Clay's waterfall enrichment for better data quality. If your sequences are leaking leads at the handoff stage, [this breakdown on sequence software gaps](/blog/2026-09-16-your-sales-sequence-software-is-leaking-leads-fix-it/) is worth reading before you scale up.

According to [G2's 2025 Sales Tools report](https://www.g2.com/categories/sales-engagement), the median sales engagement platform costs $110/seat/month for mid-market teams. This stack runs roughly $60 to $70 per seat equivalent for a solo operator, without sacrificing the automation layer that makes follow-up systematic.

The [Apollo](/tools/apollo/) plus [Smartlead](/tools/smartlead/) plus n8n combination has an upgrade path built in. When you are ready for better enrichment, drop Clay into the workflow between Apollo export and Smartlead enrollment. When you need more advanced sequencing logic, swap [n8n](/tools/n8n/) workflows for Reply.io. The Pipedrive core stays put either way.

## Running This for the Long Term

The stack pays for itself if a single meeting per month converts to a deal. At 300 contacts per month with a 3% reply rate and a 30% meeting conversion from replies, you are booking two to three meetings per month. That math works for almost any B2B sale above $5,000 ACV.

The one thing I would add at month three, once you have real data: enable Smartlead's built-in email verification waterfall before enrollment. Bounces accumulate quietly and damage your sending reputation fast. According to [Mailgun's email deliverability benchmarks](https://www.mailgun.com/email-deliverability/), keeping your hard bounce rate below 2% is the threshold that keeps you out of Google's spam bucket. Build the verification step in early. Retrofitting it after your domain reputation tanks is painful.

One more thing this stack does not include: a dialer, LinkedIn automation, or intent data. Those are real additions that cost real money. If your ICP lives on LinkedIn, [Smartlead's Starter plan does not cover it](https://smartlead.ai/pricing). Budget another $50 to $100/mo for a LinkedIn tool if you need that touchpoint.
