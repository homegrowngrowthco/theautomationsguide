## The true cost: adding it up for 10 reps

Here is the arithmetic for two realistic configurations. I call them "lean" and "full."

**Lean stack (Starter CRM, self-hosted n8n, Smartlead, Apollo Basic):**

- Apollo Basic, 10 seats: $490/mo
- Clay Pro (moderate volume): $720/mo
- n8n self-hosted: $25/mo
- Smartlead Pro: $94/mo
- HubSpot Sales Hub Starter, 10 seats: $200/mo
- **Total: $1,529/mo, or $153 per rep**

**Full stack (HubSpot Pro, cloud n8n, Instantly Hypergrowth, Apollo Pro):**

- Apollo Professional, 10 seats: $990/mo
- Clay Business (high volume): $1,400/mo
- n8n Cloud Pro: $180/mo
- Instantly Hypergrowth: $197/mo
- HubSpot Sales Hub Professional, 10 seats: $1,000/mo
- **Total: $3,767/mo, or $377 per rep**

The honest answer for most 10-rep B2B SaaS teams I work with is that they land somewhere in the middle, around $220 to $280 per rep per month. The biggest levers are Clay volume and the CRM tier decision. Push HubSpot to Starter and keep Clay on Pro with controlled enrichment triggers (which you can wire up with [n8n's enrichment workflows](/blog/2026-07-31-connect-clay-to-n8n-for-a-fully-automated-enrichment-pipelin/)) and you stay under $200 per rep without sacrificing much.

One number finance teams rarely see on the initial proposal: CRM implementation and ongoing RevOps labor. At 10 reps you typically need 0.5 to 1.0 FTE of RevOps time to keep these integrations running. At $80k to $120k loaded cost for a junior RevOps hire, that adds $333 to $500 per rep per month to the real cost. I always include this line when I'm building a board-ready budget. Always.

## Watch these cost multipliers

Three things reliably blow up RevOps budgets at 10 reps that do not show up in initial proposals.

**Clay credit overages.** The [Clay pricing page](https://www.clay.com/pricing) shows plan credit allowances, but it does not show what happens when your first big list-building campaign fires. I have watched teams burn an entire month's credit allotment in four days during a product launch push. Set a hard credit cap in Clay's settings and build an n8n alert that fires to Slack when you hit 80% of monthly credits. Build this before you need it, not after.

**Apollo credit exhaustion on shared plans.** According to [Apollo's documentation on export credits](https://knowledge.apollo.io/hc/en-us/articles/4415598752013-Apollo-Credits-Overview), export credits are pooled at the org level. One rep doing deep list pulls can starve the rest of the team. The fix is per-user credit limits in Apollo's admin settings, which most teams never configure.

**HubSpot seat creep.** Marketing, CS, and leadership all want HubSpot access eventually. At $100 per seat on Professional, each new seat is a $1,200 annual line item. Lock seat additions to a quarterly RevOps review so they do not compound silently. This is not a nice-to-have governance policy, it is a budget protection measure.

For teams managing the n8n side of this stack, the [HubSpot to n8n lead routing workflow](/blog/2026-08-02-hubspot-n8n-lead-routing-without-fillout-full-guide/) is worth reading before you start building. It covers how to avoid unnecessary HubSpot workflow triggers that push you toward higher tier requirements.

## What to take to your next budget review

A 10-rep RevOps stack built on Apollo, Clay, n8n, Smartlead or Instantly, and HubSpot realistically costs $1,500 to $3,800 per month depending on tier choices and enrichment volume. The per-rep range is $153 to $377. The single most important variable is Clay credit volume, which is usage-driven rather than seat-driven and should be modeled on your actual outbound motion cadence, not a guess. Self-hosting [n8n](/tools/n8n/) and keeping HubSpot on Starter are the two lever pulls that trim the most cost without sacrificing meaningful capability at this team size. If you are presenting this to a CFO, include a line for RevOps labor and model Clay credits at 1.5x your initial estimate until you have real volume data.
