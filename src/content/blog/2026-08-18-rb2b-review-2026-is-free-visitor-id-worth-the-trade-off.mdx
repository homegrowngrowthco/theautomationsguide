## What RB2B Actually Does (And Does Not Do)

RB2B de-anonymizes US website traffic at the person level by matching IP and browser signals against a proprietary identity graph. When a match fires, you get a Slack notification with the visitor's name, LinkedIn URL, company, and the pages they hit. That is the product. On the free tier, that is also where it stops.

No native sequence enrollment. No CRM write. No scoring. No workflow routing. You get a Slack message, and you are expected to do something human with it. For a two-person GTM team with an SDR who lives in Slack, that is actually fine. For an eight-person revenue team trying to build repeatable signal-based outbound, it becomes a manual chore almost immediately.

The paid tiers add HubSpot and Salesforce integrations, which unlocks the real automation story: visitor lands, gets written to CRM as an activity, triggers a workflow, enrolls in a sequence. That is where RB2B becomes a genuine component in a RevOps stack rather than a novelty alert. But at that point you are paying for infrastructure that Warmly or Vector also provide, often with more signal richness. My honest take is that most teams buying RB2B paid should have benchmarked Warmly first.

One thing I respect: RB2B does not bury its identification-rate limitations. The [RB2B documentation](https://www.rb2b.com) is upfront that US-only coverage is a structural constraint of their identity graph, not a bug they are fixing. That honesty is rare in this category.

## How to Choose

Start with two questions: where does your traffic come from, and how mature is your outbound process?

If more than 30 percent of your web traffic is non-US, [RB2B](/tools/rb2b/) is the wrong foundation. The identification rate on international traffic is too low to build a workflow around. Go straight to [Vector](/tools/vector/) or Warmly. This is not a close call.

If you are US-focused and under 5,000 monthly visitors, the free tier is genuinely the right call. You will identify 1,000 to 2,000 of those visitors per month, get LinkedIn URLs in Slack, and your SDR can triage manually without any automation overhead. The marginal cost of a more sophisticated tool is not justified at that scale.

Where I see teams consistently err is staying on RB2B too long. The moment you are clearing 20-plus visitor alerts per day and your SDR is spending an hour triaging them, you have outgrown the tool. That is the exact trigger to move to Warmly and build the [signal-based outbound workflow](/blog/2026-08-16-warmly-make-signal-based-outbound-workflow-guide/) that actually scales. And if you want to score those visitors against social buying signals, adding [Trigify alongside a HubSpot lead scoring automation](/blog/2026-08-15-trigify-hubspot-lead-scoring-automation-2026-guide/) is the upgrade path, not a replacement for visitor ID.

<MyTake>
The "free" positioning is doing a lot of heavy lifting for RB2B. The real product is the paid CRM integration tier, and at that price point you should benchmark it against Warmly before signing anything. I have had clients stick with RB2B paid because the simplicity was a feature, not a bug. Those were smaller teams where Warmly's orchestration complexity would have been overhead, not value. But I have also watched teams at the 15-rep stage treat RB2B as a permanent solution, then wonder why their SDRs are burning 90 minutes a day on manual triage. ## The Verdict on RB2B in 2026

RB2B earns its reputation as the lowest-friction entry point into visitor identification. For US-focused teams, the free tier is not a gimmick. It genuinely works. But it is a starting point, not a destination.

The absence of built-in workflow logic, the US-only coverage constraint, and the thin enrichment story mean most teams will either stay small enough that they never need more, or they will graduate to a platform with real orchestration capability. Use it to prove the concept internally and build the internal budget case for something that scales. The free tier will not hurt you. Treating it as a permanent infrastructure decision probably will. According to [G2's 2026 buyer intent category data](https://www.g2.com/categories/buyer-intent-data-tools), teams that invest in signal enrichment on top of basic visitor ID see materially higher conversion rates from identified traffic, which is exactly the gap RB2B leaves open.
