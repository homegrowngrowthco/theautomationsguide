## How to choose

Start with channels, not features. If LinkedIn is a first-touch channel in your sequences, you can immediately rule out Artisan until their roadmap restores that capability. That narrows the field to AiSDR and 11x for most multi-channel teams.

The decision between those two comes down to operator control. AiSDR is the pick when a RevOps practitioner owns the workflow day-to-day. You can read and edit the sequence rules, the CRM sync is clean, and personalization prompts are fully visible. 11x makes more sense when you have an enterprise buyer who wants to hand the keys to the agent and review pipeline weekly rather than inspect cadence logic at the step level.

I would also pressure-test CRM sync before signing any of these. Ask for a demo environment where you can watch a replied contact update a lifecycle stage in your actual HubSpot or Salesforce org in real time. Every vendor will say "yes we integrate," but the fidelity varies significantly. The tools I've seen fail hardest in production are the ones where the AI takes an action (reply received, meeting booked) and the CRM finds out through a nightly batch sync instead of a live webhook. That lag creates duplicate sequences, confusion for AEs, and data you simply cannot trust.

<MyTake>
"AI SDR" is doing a lot of marketing work right now. What most of these tools actually deliver is AI-assisted SDR, not autonomous replacement. I tell every client to budget for one human SDR who manages the agent's quality, handles complex replies, and owns ICP tuning. Teams that go fully hands-off in month one almost always hit deliverability or reply-quality problems by month three. The agent is the engine. You still need a driver.

## The honest bottom line

The productivity gains here are real. So is the platform risk. Artisan's LinkedIn removal in early 2026 is a direct reminder that when you build outbound infrastructure on top of a third-party agent, that agent's platform dependencies become your dependencies. [AiSDR](/go/aisdr/) is where I'd start for most B2B SaaS teams: the CRM integration is the most mature, the sequence logic is auditable, and the channel surface (email plus LinkedIn) is intact. [11x](/go/11x/) is worth a conversation at enterprise scale when maximum autonomy is the priority. [Artisan](/go/artisan/) is a watch-and-wait unless your program is email-only and UX polish matters more to your team than channel breadth.
