## What each tool actually does in 2026

Before scoring these against each other, be precise about the job each tool was built for. Surfer and Frase are SEO optimization tools. They analyze SERPs, score your content against competitors, suggest NLP terms, and help you produce pages that rank in blue-link Google results. Mature, well-integrated, and genuinely good at that job.

[Profound](/tools/profound/) is something different. It is a [GEO (generative engine optimization)](https://searchengineland.com/generative-engine-optimization-guide-445418) platform that monitors how AI systems cite and describe your brand, tracks your share of voice inside AI-generated answers, and surfaces recommendations for making your content more likely to be pulled into those answers. It connects to ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews and gives you a dashboard showing how often your brand appears, what competitors appear instead, and which content changes actually shifted your citation rate. I covered the traditional SEO side of this comparison in more depth in the [Surfer vs Frase vs Clearscope](/blog/2026-07-11-surfer-vs-frase-vs-clearscope-best-for-geo-in-2026/) post, worth reading alongside this one.

My clients in martech and sales tech categories are already seeing Perplexity route real purchase-intent queries, and the brands showing up in those answers are not always the ones with the best Surfer content scores. That disconnect is the whole reason Profound matters.

## How to choose

This is not really a three-way competition, and framing it that way leads teams to the wrong conclusion. Surfer and Frase compete directly for the same buyer: a content or SEO team trying to rank on Google. Profound is operating in a separate category. The real decision is whether GEO has crossed the importance threshold for your team yet.

For most B2B SaaS teams I work with, the answer in mid-2026 is yes, but carefully. AI-driven search is a real channel. [Google's AI Overviews are expanding](https://blog.google/products/search/google-search-generative-experience-expansion/) to more query types weekly, and Perplexity is routing real commercial-intent queries that used to land on your blog. If you are in a competitive SaaS category (sales tech, martech, HR tech), your competitors are already thinking about this.

Between Surfer and Frase specifically: Surfer wins on content grading depth and integration polish. [Frase](/tools/frase/) wins on price-to-value for lean teams doing mostly brief creation. [Surfer](/tools/surfer/) wins if your writers need sentence-level guidance while they draft. Don't buy Surfer for brief creation and don't buy Frase expecting a full NLP scoring engine. They each do one thing best.

## The stack play most teams should run right now

If you are a B2B SaaS content or GTM team serious about search in 2026, this is not an either/or question. The practical answer is Profound for GEO monitoring and AI citation tracking, plus whichever of Surfer or Frase you already use for classic SEO. They do not overlap. Profound will not write your content briefs. Surfer will not tell you if ChatGPT is citing a competitor when someone asks "what is the best tool in your category."

The analogy I use with clients: Surfer is your on-page SEO tool, Profound is your brand monitoring tool for a new channel. You would not cancel Gong because you bought a new CRM. Same logic applies here. Profound is additive until AI search drives enough of your pipeline to justify rethinking the whole content strategy around it.

That day is coming faster than most RevOps and content leaders expect. The teams building GEO habits and citation baselines now will be far better positioned when it does.
