## The true cost: real math for a 10-seat team

Let me walk through what a 10-person SDR team actually pays on [JustCall](/go/justcall/) in 2026.

The **Pro plan** is $49 per seat per month billed annually. That is the minimum tier where you get [HubSpot](/tools/hubspot/) and [Salesforce](/tools/salesforce/) auto-logging, click-to-dial, and the call recording your compliance team will ask for on day one. Ten seats: $490 per month, $5,880 per year before anything else touches the invoice.

Then the real line items arrive.

**JustCall AI add-on:** Transcription, AI call scoring, moment detection, and coaching insights. Approximately $30 per seat per month. Ten seats adds $300 per month, running total $790/month.

**SMS credits:** JustCall does not bundle SMS into voice plans. You buy credits separately. A team sending 2,000 outbound SMS per month (modest for an SDR team) pays roughly $40 to $80 per month depending on whether you pre-purchase a bundle or pay on demand. Call it $60 on average.

**International calling:** If your team dials outside the US and Canada, per-minute rates apply. UK numbers run around $0.03/min outbound. EU landlines can hit $0.07/min. One rep making 50 international calls per day at three minutes average burns $4.50 per day. Scale that across 10 reps and 20 working days: $900/month in overage alone. This is the line item that shocks people. Every time.

**Realistic all-in for 10 seats (US-only calling):** $850 to $950 per month.

**Realistic all-in for 10 seats (mixed international):** $1,400 to $1,800 per month.

The [JustCall pricing page](https://justcall.io/pricing) lists the per-seat numbers clearly, but SMS and international costs live in separate rate card documentation that most buyers never find until the first invoice lands.

## How Aloware fits into this picture

[Aloware](/go/aloware/) competes in the same mid-market segment and prices differently from everyone else here. Aloware bundles unlimited local calling into its base plan (starting around $30/seat), which essentially eliminates the per-minute surprise. For teams with high US call volume, that bundled model can actually come out cheaper than JustCall Pro once you account for JustCall's per-minute overages.

The tradeoff is real though. Aloware's AI features are less mature than JustCall's as of mid-2026. Their [HubSpot](/go/hubspot/) integration is functional but requires more manual configuration. I've set it up for two clients and both needed a half-day of ops work to get activity sync reliable. The [Aloware](/tools/aloware/) support team responds fast, but the product is clearly earlier-stage than JustCall on the integration front.

My recommendation: if your team does high-volume US-only calling and predictable flat-rate billing matters more than AI coaching depth, get a quote from Aloware. If your reps dial internationally or you want AI coaching baked in from day one, JustCall's depth justifies the higher ceiling cost.

## Where the pricing is fair, and where it stings

<MyTake>
JustCall's AI add-on is actually worth the $30 per seat. I've benchmarked it against standalone coaching tools at three clients and it holds up surprisingly well against Gong for teams under 25 reps. What I can't defend is the SMS credit model. Selling voice and SMS credits separately in 2026, when Aircall and Aloware have both moved toward bundled messaging, is a deliberate choice to keep the headline price low while burying real costs. My clients consistently underestimate their first three months of SMS spend by 35 to 50 percent. Budget SMS as a separate line item from day one or you will get a bad surprise.
</MyTake>

The [G2 reviews for JustCall](https://www.g2.com/products/justcall/reviews) consistently surface two themes: strong satisfaction with AI features, and repeated complaints about billing surprises on international and SMS. That pattern maps exactly to what I see in client evaluations. The product team is building well. The pricing architecture has not caught up with the product quality.

If you're evaluating dialers as part of a broader RevOps stack audit, the right comparison is not JustCall vs. Aircall on seat price alone. It's JustCall (with AI included) vs. Aircall plus a separate coaching tool like [Gong](/go/gong/). On that full-stack math, JustCall often wins, especially for teams under 25 seats where Gong's minimum contract is genuinely painful.
