## Why teams are churning off Brevo for cold email

Brevo's value proposition for cold email is essentially: it's cheap and I already have it. That logic holds until reply rates drop and you start wondering whether your messages are landing in primary tabs. The core problem is structural. [Brevo](/tools/brevo/) routes outbound through shared IP pools tuned for opted-in marketing mail, not prospecting at scale. No inbox rotation. No per-domain warmup scheduler. No deliverability dashboard telling you which sending accounts are healthy.

The API, while well-documented per the [Brevo developer reference](https://developers.brevo.com/docs), is designed around campaign sends and transactional triggers, not the sequence-step-level controls that RevOps teams need to wire cold email into a [Clay](/go/clay/) or n8n workflow. If you're running anything above a few hundred cold sends per day, you're leaving deliverability performance on the table.

I'd argue the lack of a unified bounce and blacklist monitor is the most dangerous gap. Teams using Brevo for cold outreach often don't realize a domain is burned until reply rates collapse. By then, you've torched sender reputation that takes weeks to recover.

## How to choose between these three

The decision almost always comes down to one question: do you need multichannel, or do you need maximum deliverability at email volume?

Pure cold email at scale? Instantly wins on infrastructure. Unlimited inboxes, a warmup pool of 200k+ accounts per their [pricing page](https://instantly.ai/pricing), and a sending algorithm built around inbox placement make it the default for most outbound-heavy GTM teams. My read is that it's the closest thing to an obvious swap for anyone currently on Brevo. Easiest migration path, strongest deliverability defaults, and the Growth plan starts at $37/month, which isn't dramatically more than Brevo Starter once you account for the dedicated sending infrastructure you're buying.

For agencies or teams managing multiple client accounts, [Smartlead](/tools/smartlead/) edges ahead on workspace organization and API depth. If you're piping leads programmatically from Clay or an n8n enrichment workflow, Smartlead's sequence-level endpoints give you controls that Brevo simply doesn't expose. The [Instantly vs. Smartlead deliverability comparison](/blog/2026-10-06-instantly-vs-smartlead-deliverability-domains-roi/) covers that head-to-head in more depth if you're deciding between those two specifically.

[Lemlist](/go/lemlist/) is the right pick when cold email is one channel in a broader outbound motion. If your sequence needs to touch LinkedIn between email steps, or your AEs want a call task mid-sequence, Lemlist handles that in one place. The personalized visual assets (custom image overlays, video thumbnails) are genuinely differentiated and perform well targeting personas who receive a lot of plain-text cold email. The tradeoff: higher cost per seat and a thinner deliverability story at high volume compared to Instantly.

One more thing worth naming. If the reason you're on Brevo is that your newsletter and cold email live in the same tool, you'll need to separate those workflows when you switch. Instantly, Smartlead, and Lemlist are cold outreach tools, full stop. For the newsletter side, the [Kit vs. Beehiiv vs. Brevo newsletter comparison](/blog/2026-10-06-kit-vs-beehiiv-vs-brevo-newsletter-comparison-2026/) is worth reading before you fully cancel Brevo.

## The bottom line on switching

Brevo is not a bad product. It's a mismatched product for cold email. Shared SMTP infrastructure, no inbox rotation, no native warmup, and a sequence-unaware API are structural limitations, not bugs that will get patched in the next release. If cold outreach is a meaningful part of your GTM motion, moving to a purpose-built tool isn't optional.

For most teams, [Instantly](/tools/instantly/) is the right first call: easiest migration, strongest deliverability defaults, and a price point that's not dramatically higher than Brevo once you factor in the mailboxes you're already managing separately. If you're a smaller team evaluating Instantly against lighter options, the [Woodpecker vs. Instantly comparison for small teams](/blog/2026-10-05-woodpecker-vs-instantly-best-cold-email-for-small-teams/) is a useful reference before you commit.
