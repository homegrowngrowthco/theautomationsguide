## Before you migrate

Do not touch Smartlead until you have done this prep inside Lemlist. I have watched teams spin up new campaigns in Smartlead while Lemlist was still sending, and the result is double-sends, angry prospects, and a deliverability mess that takes months to unwind.

Here is the pre-migration checklist:

- **Pause every active Lemlist sequence.** Go to Campaigns, filter by "Active," and pause each one. Do not delete them yet.
- **Export your contact lists.** For each campaign, export leads as CSV. The file includes email, first name, company, current step, and reply status.
- **Export sequence copy.** There is no bulk export for step content. Open each campaign, copy the subject lines and body text into a Google Doc. Yes, this is manual. Do it anyway.
- **Screenshot your sending schedule settings.** Days, times, timezone, throttle limits. You will recreate these in Smartlead.
- **Audit your sending domains.** Pull a list of every domain you have authenticated in Lemlist. Check them against [MXToolbox's blacklist lookup](https://mxtoolbox.com/blacklists.aspx) before deciding which ones to bring forward.
- **Note contacts mid-sequence.** Anyone past Step 1 but without a reply needs special handling. Either let them finish in Lemlist (daily volume paused, just let the sequence complete naturally) or tag them and suppress from Smartlead until they go cold.
- **Download your analytics.** Lemlist's reporting is not cleanly exportable after you cancel. Grab screenshots or CSV exports of open and reply rates by campaign before you close the account.

## What breaks and what does not transfer

Two things consistently catch teams off guard: LinkedIn steps and video personalization.

Lemlist has native LinkedIn connection request and message steps built into sequences. Smartlead does not. If your sequences were multichannel, you need to either rebuild the LinkedIn touchpoints in a separate tool (most clients I work with run a [Clay](/tools/clay/) workflow to trigger LinkedIn tasks in parallel) or accept that you are running email-only sequences in Smartlead for now. That trade-off is real. Plan for it before you migrate, not after.

Lemlist's video personalization feature (the dynamic intro video thumbnails) also has no Smartlead equivalent. If that was doing work for your reply rates, you need to compensate with copy quality and tighter list segmentation, not a feature swap.

The [n8n](/tools/n8n/) webhook workflow is also worth testing carefully before you go live. Smartlead's webhook payload includes a `reply_email_body` field, but it sends raw HTML. Strip those tags in your n8n expression before writing the reply body to any CRM field. A simple regex replace node handles this in about 30 seconds.

## Verify it worked

Before you declare the migration complete, run through these four checks:

1. **Inbox placement test.** Send a test sequence to a seed list using MailReach or GlockApps. Confirm inbox rate is above 90% across Gmail, Outlook, and your prospects' primary providers.
2. **Reply detection fires correctly.** Reply to one of your test emails from a personal inbox. Confirm the n8n workflow triggers, logs to your CRM, and creates the follow-up task. Check it twice: once for a contact that already exists in HubSpot and once for one that does not.
3. **Bounce handling works.** Send to one known invalid address and confirm it hits the bounce bucket in Smartlead without retrying. Verify your global blocklist was updated automatically.
4. **No double-sends from Lemlist.** Log into Lemlist and confirm all campaigns are still paused. Cross-reference the contact list in Smartlead against recent Lemlist activity to make sure nobody is sitting in both active queues.

## Getting back to full volume

The migration itself is a weekend project. The hard part is the 10 to 14 day warm-up window, and that is exactly what teams consistently try to shortcut. Every client I have seen blast full volume before warm-up completes has ended up in spam folders and had to restart from scratch. Respect the ramp. Use that time to audit your list quality, tighten your copy, and get the [n8n](/tools/n8n/) CRM sync working so that when replies do start coming in, they land in your pipeline automatically.

Smartlead without CRM sync is still a manual reply-tracking operation. With the webhook workflow running, every reply triggers a task, moves a deal stage, and creates an audit trail. For any RevOps team operating at scale, that is not optional. Per [Google's email sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126), maintaining clean sending practices and prompt reply handling is also increasingly a deliverability factor, not just a sales hygiene one. Wire it up on day one.
