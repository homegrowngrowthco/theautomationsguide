## What each tool is actually built for

These four tools are not direct substitutes. They solve adjacent problems. Picking the wrong one for your workflow is the most common failure I see in RevOps evaluations, and it usually comes down to confusing a prospecting tool with an enrichment tool.

## How to choose

The first thing I'd check is whether your enrichment need is synchronous or asynchronous. That single question eliminates most of the confusion. If reps are doing live prospecting on LinkedIn, Surfe is the only tool in this group built for that motion. The real-time CRM push alone eliminates a manual step that compounds badly at scale.

If you are enriching a list before loading it into [Smartlead](/go/smartlead/) or [Instantly](/go/instantly/), FullEnrich's batch waterfall will get you higher email coverage per credit spent. The API pairs cleanly with [n8n](/go/n8n/) or [Make](/go/make/) workflows for anyone already running automated enrichment pipelines (see [our guide on Make integrations for GTM teams](/blog/2026-10-14-make-integrations-that-actually-work-for-gtm-teams-in-2026/)). I'd argue this is the default choice for any team running structured outbound at volume, not a decision that requires much deliberation.

Phone number coverage is the other real differentiator. FullEnrich and Surfe are primarily email-first. If your outbound motion involves cold calling or a multi-channel sequence that hits both email and phone, [BetterContact](/tools/bettercontact/) deserves serious consideration. [G2 reviewers consistently flag](https://www.g2.com/categories/data-enrichment) mobile number hit rate as BetterContact's primary advantage over the email-focused waterfall tools. For teams that only need occasional enrichment and do not want to commit to a monthly seat cost, [LeadMagic](/tools/leadmagic/) keeps the cost floor low. No subscription pressure, no minimum commitment.

<MyTake>
The "Surfe vs FullEnrich" framing is a category error. Surfe is a prospecting tool that happens to do enrichment. FullEnrich is an enrichment tool that does nothing else. Most outbound-focused teams with more than three SDRs end up running both: FullEnrich for batch list work, Surfe for live LinkedIn prospecting. Treating them as an either/or decision is how teams end up under-covered on one half of their workflow.
</MyTake>

## The coverage question answered directly

On raw waterfall depth for bulk email enrichment, [FullEnrich](/tools/fullenrich/) has the edge. More providers in the cascade means more chances to surface a verified email, and for outbound-heavy teams sending high volumes, that coverage percentage compounds into meaningful deliverability gains. For teams whose entire SDR motion runs through LinkedIn, [Surfe](/tools/surfe/) workflow integration advantage outweighs the marginal coverage gap. BetterContact is the specialist pick for phone-first or multi-channel outbound. LeadMagic is the budget entry point that does not try to be anything else. None of them replace [Clay](/tools/clay/) for complex enrichment logic, but for teams that do not need Clay's full power, these tools offer a lighter-weight path to coverage that is worth the evaluation time.
