## What Woodpecker actually charges

[Woodpecker's pricing page](https://woodpecker.co/pricing/) structures billing around two axes: plan tier and mailbox count. The Cold Email plan covers outbound prospecting sequences. The Sales Assistant plan adds CRM-style follow-up tracking. There is also an Agency plan for multi-client management.

Here is how the tiers break down in plain language:

**Cold Email (from $29/mailbox/month):** Basic sequence builder, A/B testing, deliverability monitoring, and 500 contacted prospects per month on the entry configuration. Need more contacts? You buy up to a higher prospect tier. The per-mailbox charge stays constant regardless of how many sequences you run.

**Sales Assistant (from $49/mailbox/month):** Adds CRM task management, prospect scoring, and a higher default prospect limit. This is where agencies and SDR teams with active pipeline management typically land. The price jump per mailbox adds up fast.

**Agency (custom):** White-label client management, team seats, and consolidated billing across clients. Priced on volume and usually requires a conversation with sales. The first thing I'd check in any agency deal is whether the per-mailbox rate is negotiated or stays at list price.

A few add-ons worth flagging: email verification is not native, so you are paying for an integration (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, or Bouncer) on top of your plan. Warmup runs through Woodpecker's own warmup pool, but it is not as configurable as what [Smartlead](/go/smartlead/) or Instantly ship natively.

## The true cost for a real outbound team

Say a five-person SDR team each runs two active sending addresses. That is a realistic configuration for inbox rotation and deliverability. Ten mailboxes on the Cold Email plan.

**Woodpecker:** 10 mailboxes x $29 = $290/month. Add a ZeroBounce plan for verification (roughly $20-30/month at moderate volume) and you are at $310-320/month before any CRM connectors or agency features.

**Same team on Instantly's Hypergrowth plan ($358/month):** Unlimited mailboxes, built-in warmup across all inboxes, and unlimited active leads. Pricier at that specific count, but the ceiling disappears. At fifteen mailboxes, Instantly is already cheaper per mailbox.

**Same team on [Smartlead](/tools/smartlead/)'s Pro plan ($94/month):** 60 active mailboxes, unlimited email sending, and AI warmup included. For teams that have already moved past five inboxes, Smartlead is the most aggressive value in the market right now. It is not close.

My read: the math only favors Woodpecker at one to three mailboxes. Past that, the per-mailbox billing is a slow cost leak that compounds every time you add an inbox.

## Where Woodpecker holds its ground

To be fair: Woodpecker is not overpriced for what it delivers. The deliverability monitoring is genuinely useful. The sequence builder is stable and mature. The agency workflow (client-separated campaigns, white-label reports) has no direct equivalent in [Instantly](/tools/instantly/) at the same price point. Per [G2 reviews on Woodpecker](https://www.g2.com/products/woodpecker/reviews), users consistently praise deliverability and support responsiveness. Those things matter more than a $30/month difference when a campaign is actively running.

The CRM integrations, while Zapier-dependent for most connections, cover the common stack. Running Pipedrive or HubSpot sequences that trigger into Woodpecker via webhook? That pattern works reliably. The trouble starts when you need anything more complex: routing warm replies into a CRM stage, triggering enrichment on open, or building conditional branches. That requires glue you build and maintain yourself. Compare that to Smartlead's native webhooks and you start to see where the product has aged.

For agencies managing ten or more clients with separate inboxes per client, Woodpecker's agency plan is still one of the cleaner operational models. Stitching together sub-accounts in Instantly works, but it lacks the reporting layer agencies need for client-facing dashboards.

If you are evaluating how Woodpecker compares directly against Instantly on feature parity, the [Woodpecker vs Instantly comparison](/blog/2026-10-05-woodpecker-vs-instantly-best- ) covers that match-up in detail.

## The honest switching calculus

The switch trigger is rarely frustration with Woodpecker itself. The usual pattern is staying too long because "it works," while the mailbox count quietly doubles and the bill follows. My first check for any team evaluating this: count your active sending addresses, multiply by $29, and compare that number to Instantly's Growth plan or Smartlead's Pro. If Woodpecker is more than $50/month cheaper, stay. If it is within $30, the feature set differences probably tip toward the flat-rate tools.

One practical note worth flagging: [Woodpecker does not export sequences in a format that imports cleanly elsewhere](/blog/2026-10-10-migrate-from-brevo-to-smartlead-step-by-step-playbook/), so budget migration time if you have long, multi-step sequences. Contacts export as CSV without issue. Sequences need to be rebuilt manually.
