The trigger I see most often is a deliverability cliff. Teams run [Apollo](/go/apollo/) hard for six months, reply rates drift from 4% down to 1.5%, and someone finally checks the spam folder to find half the sequences never landed in inbox. At that point, switching sequencers feels urgent. The trap is rushing the export and recreating the same problems in a new tool. Done right, an Apollo to Smartlead migration takes about a week of prep and preserves everything that matters: your suppression list, your contact history, and your domain reputation.

## Before you migrate

Do not touch the destination until the source is fully documented. I've watched teams delete Apollo campaigns the moment they signed up for [Smartlead](/go/smartlead/), then spend two weeks trying to reconstruct suppression lists from memory. Spend a half-day here. It saves a month of headaches.

**Pull these artifacts from Apollo before anything else:**

- All contact exports, segmented by campaign (Apollo lets you filter by sequence, then export to CSV per sequence, not as one giant file)
- The "do not contact" list under Settings > Do Not Contact, exported as CSV
- Activity logs for every contact you care about (Apollo activity is exportable at the contact level under People > export with activity columns checked)
- Your sequence copy: subject lines, body text, wait days, and send conditions for every active and recently paused sequence
- DNS records you authenticated in Apollo: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC entries, plus which inbox accounts were connected

If you also ran campaigns in [Instantly](/go/instantly/) and are consolidating into Smartlead, [that migration has its own contact-data quirks](/blog/2026-09-27-migrate-apollo-to-instantly-without-losing-contact-data/) worth reviewing first.

## What breaks and what does not transfer

Most teams are surprised by three things.

**Sending history is the biggest loss.** Apollo stores reply, click, open, and bounce events per contact. Smartlead has no import endpoint for historical engagement data. Your workaround: before you cut over, export Apollo's contact activity with the engagement columns and push that data into your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, whatever you use). Tag contacts as "Apollo engaged" or "Apollo bounced" in the CRM. The history lives somewhere queryable even if Smartlead never sees it.

**Merge fields use different syntax.** Apollo uses `{{first_name}}` style tokens. Smartlead uses `{{FIRST_NAME}}` (uppercase by default in most versions). Check every sequence step after rebuilding and normalize the field tokens, or your emails will render the raw placeholder instead of the personalization. If you want to catch broken merge fields before they go live, [Smartlead's deliverability diagnostics](/blog/2026-09-29-diagnose-cold-email-deliverability-gaps-with-smartlead/) surface rendering errors in test sends. I make this check a hard gate before any campaign goes live.

## Verify it worked

Four concrete checks before you call the migration complete:

1. **Blocklist integrity.** Send a test import of 5 known do-not-contact emails. Smartlead should reject them at the campaign level. If they pass through, your blocklist upload did not take correctly.
2. **Authentication passing.** In Smartlead's Email Accounts view, every connected inbox should show a green checkmark on SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. Red means DNS propagation is incomplete.
3. **Warmup running.** Check the warmup dashboard in Smartlead for each inbox. You should see warmup emails going out and receiving positive engagement signals. If warmup shows zero activity after 24 hours, the inbox is not properly connected.
4. **Test campaign send.** Create a one-step campaign with a seed list of inboxes you control (use [mail-tester.com](https://www.mail-tester.com/) or your own test addresses across Gmail and Outlook). Confirm the email renders correctly, merge fields resolve, and the email hits inbox rather than spam.

<MyTake>
Most teams do not actually have a sequencer problem. They have a domain reputation problem that no tool switch will fix. Run a full deliverability audit with fresh test sends from MXToolbox and mail-tester.com before migrating anything. If your sending domains are already burned, you need new domains regardless of which sequencer you land on. Smartlead's warmup is genuinely better than Apollo's, but it cannot resurrect a domain Google has already flagged. Fix the infrastructure first. Migrate the contacts second.
</MyTake>

## One week is enough if you sequence the work correctly

This migration is not technically hard. It is sequencing work: do the exports and blocklist prep before you touch Smartlead, warm inboxes before you import contacts, verify authentication before you hit send. The teams that have a painful time treat it as a same-day CSV import. If you need to enrich your contact list during the migration, [Clay](/tools/clay/) fits cleanly into this workflow before the Smartlead import step. And if you are running a multi-step automation to keep Apollo sourcing flowing into Smartlead campaigns, [n8n](/tools/n8n/) is the orchestration layer I'd use. It handles Apollo API pagination and rate limits far better than Zapier does at this volume.
