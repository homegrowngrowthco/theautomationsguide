## How to Choose

The first thing I'd check is your dialing motion. If your SDRs are running 80-plus dials per day and connect rate is the primary KPI, Kixie's parallel dialer is a material productivity lever, not a marginal one. The automation triggers that fire Kixie sessions from a [Salesforce](/tools/salesforce/) or [HubSpot](/tools/hubspot/) workflow close a loop that most teams are currently closing manually with a copy-paste list. That's a real operational cost, and it compounds daily.

If you're running a blended outbound motion (calls plus SMS, calls plus cold email sequences from [Smartlead](/tools/smartlead/) or similar), JustCall's breadth of integrations and native SMS sequences make it the operationally simpler choice. It's also the right call if your CRM is [Pipedrive](/tools/pipedrive/) or anything outside the Salesforce-HubSpot duopoly, where Aircall's integration quality tends to drop off noticeably.

Aircall earns its place when compliance, call quality, or a hybrid rep team (some field, some desk) is in play. It is not the right tool for a pure-volume SDR floor. I'd argue most teams that land on Aircall are optimizing for the wrong variable: they pick the prettiest UI when they should be picking the tightest CRM loop.

<MyTake>
Conventional wisdom ranks Aircall above JustCall on overall quality. I'd push back hard on that for outbound-heavy teams. Aircall's polish is real, but polish does not move pipe. JustCall's integration surface and native SMS capabilities make it a better RevOps citizen in a modern GTM stack, and the price difference compounds fast at team scale. Aircall is priced for a compliance and reliability problem that most SDR teams simply do not have.
</MyTake>

## The Bottom Line

For most SDR teams in 2026, JustCall is the right default. Broad integrations, competitive pricing, and enough dialer functionality for teams running under 60 dials per day per rep. Step up to Kixie if parallel dialing and CRM-triggered automation are non-negotiable for your motion. Choose Aircall if call quality, compliance, or UX consistency across a hybrid team is the deciding factor. All three are legitimate options. The gap is in which operational constraint you're actually solving for.
