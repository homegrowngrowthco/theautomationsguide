I've watched this exact sequence play out with three clients in the last six months. The frustrating part isn't the decision to leave. It's the two weeks of limbo where sequences are paused, DNS records are half-migrated, and someone watches reply rates fall off a cliff. This guide is a playbook for doing the switch cleanly, so you land in [Instantly](/go/instantly/) with your sending domains intact and your pipeline moving.

## Before You Migrate: The Prep Checklist

Skipping this section is how teams lose two weeks of sending. Do this work before you cancel [Saleshandy](/tools/saleshandy/) or touch a DNS record.

- **Export all active contacts.** Go to Saleshandy's Prospects section, filter by campaign, and export each list as a CSV. Include custom field columns or you will rebuild that data from scratch.
- **Document your sequences.** There is no native export for sequence steps. Open each active sequence and copy the subject lines, body copy, wait days, and step counts into a Google Doc or Notion page. I screenshot every step and drop them into a shared Drive folder. Tedious, but it takes maybe 20 minutes and saves you from guessing later.
- **Note your sending limits per mailbox.** Saleshandy enforces sending caps differently than Instantly. Document how many emails per day each connected mailbox was sending. You will use this to configure Instantly's ramp-up settings.
- **Identify which domains are actively sending.** Any domain sending more than 100 emails per day needs a longer warmup runway in the new tool. Flag those separately.
- **Pause (do not delete) active sequences.** Pausing preserves your place in each contact's step history. If a reply comes in during the migration window, you will know where they were in the flow.
- **Keep Saleshandy active until Instantly is fully configured.** Canceling early is how you end up with a two-week gap in sending.

## What Breaks and What Does Not Transfer

This is the section I wish someone had handed me the first time I ran this migration for a client.

<SideBySide>
  <Fragment slot="left">
    **What breaks or does not transfer:**
    - Historical open, click, and reply rate data. Saleshandy stores this internally with no export format Instantly ingests.
    - Email account warmup scores. Your warmup reputation is tied to Saleshandy's sending network. You start at zero in Instantly's warmup pool.
    - Sequence step progress for mid-funnel contacts. If a contact was on step 3 of 5, Instantly has no way to know that. You have to manually decide whether to re-enroll them at step 3 or start over.
    - Any Saleshandy-native merge tags using non-standard field names. These render as blank or as raw field names in Instantly until you remap them.
    - Bounce and unsubscribe suppression lists. Export your unsubscribe list from Saleshandy and upload it to Instantly's block list on day one. Missing this step is a compliance risk.
  </Fragment>

The suppression list issue bites people badly, and I've seen it firsthand. One team I worked with had 400 Saleshandy unsubscribes that never made it into Instantly. They re-emailed a chunk of them in week one and caught a compliance complaint within 48 hours. Export that list on day zero. Not day two. Day zero.

## Verify It Worked

Before you call the migration complete, run through these four checks:

1. **MXToolbox or [mail-tester.com](https://www.mail-tester.com) on each sending domain.** Confirm SPF includes Instantly's sending IP range, DKIM passes with Instantly's selector, and DMARC is reporting rather than blocking during the transition window.
2. **Send a test email from each migrated mailbox to a Gmail and an Outlook address you control.** Open the raw headers and confirm the `d=` field in the DKIM signature matches your domain, not Saleshandy's.
3. **Check Instantly's warmup dashboard after 48 hours.** You should see warmup sends going out and positive engagement signals (opens, replies from the warmup pool) registering. If the warmup counter is stuck at zero, the mailbox connection is broken.
4. **Confirm your unsubscribe block list is active.** Import your Saleshandy suppression export, go to Instantly's block list settings, and verify the count matches. Spot-check two or three addresses to confirm they would be blocked from new campaigns.

<MyTake>
The "just re-warm your mailboxes" advice undersells the real risk. The teams I've seen blow up a domain during migration are not the ones who skip warmup entirely. They are the ones who run warmup for five days, get impatient, and push full cold volume before the warmup pool has built enough positive signal. Two weeks is the floor, not a conservative cushion. Treat it as non-negotiable.

## One Sprint, Clean Migration

A Saleshandy to Instantly migration is a three-to-four week project. Not a weekend job. Week one is exports, docs, and DNS. Week two is Instantly account setup and warmup activation. Weeks three and four are the warmup runway before you push any cold volume. Compress this into a long weekend and you will lose domain reputation that took months to earn.

Do the prep. Respect the warmup window. Re-verify your suppression lists. You will come out the other side with lower per-mailbox costs, better deliverability tooling, and a sending infrastructure that scales horizontally without seat-based pricing walls.

For teams also evaluating [Smartlead](/tools/smartlead/) as their destination, the warmup and DNS steps are nearly identical. The main difference is sequence rebuild time. Smartlead's campaign builder has more conditional branching options, which take longer to configure from scratch.
