## Before You Migrate

The prep work is where migrations actually succeed or fail. Rushing into Smartlead setup without a clean export is the most common failure mode in cold email stack switches. I'd put the suppression list export at the top of the list, above everything else, because it's the one mistake you cannot undo after the fact.

Do all of this before you touch Smartlead:

- **Export all contact lists** from Brevo as CSV, including every custom field column you use in personalization tokens
- **Export your suppression/unsubscribe list** separately (Brevo calls this "Blocklist" under Contacts > Blocklist). This is not included in standard list exports
- **Screenshot or copy every active sequence**: subject lines, body copy, wait days, send conditions. Brevo does not export sequences as importable files
- **Audit your sending domains**: note every domain you have authenticated in Brevo (Settings > Senders and IP > Domains)
- **Pause, do not delete, any active campaigns** in Brevo. Deleting loses the thread history and reply tracking
- **Check your current DNS records**: pull SPF, DKIM, and DMARC for each domain using [MXToolbox](https://mxtoolbox.com) before you change anything

If you have been using Brevo for transactional email alongside cold outreach, keep Brevo active for transactional until you have a dedicated transactional provider standing by. Postmark or Resend are the two I'd point you toward. [Smartlead](/go/smartlead/) is not a transactional platform, full stop.

## What Breaks and What Does Not Transfer

The merge syntax difference catches almost every team mid-migration. Brevo uses double curly brace syntax for personalization. Smartlead uses a similar but distinct format. Paste Brevo templates directly into Smartlead without updating variable names and every email goes out with a raw unparsed token where the first name should be. Don't do it.

Other things that do not transfer cleanly: A/B test results from Brevo campaigns stay in Brevo. Open rate history per contact is not portable. Any Brevo automation that triggered based on email opens or clicks needs to be rebuilt in Smartlead's campaign conditions or in your CRM workflow layer.

What does transfer cleanly: contact data (via CSV), your copywritten sequence content (manually rebuilt), and your domain authentication (once DNS is updated).

## Verify It Worked

Before you declare the migration done, run these four checks:

1. **Send a test email from each mailbox** through Smartlead to a [Mail-Tester](https://www.mail-tester.com) address and confirm you score 9 or higher. A score below 8 means an auth record is broken.
2. **Confirm suppression list coverage** by searching one known unsubscribe email in Smartlead's unsubscribe list. If it appears, the import succeeded.
3. **Check the warmup dashboard** for each mailbox: spam placement rate should be below 3% before you enable live campaign sending.
4. **Send one campaign to a small segment first** (100 to 200 contacts) and monitor replies, bounces, and spam reports for 48 hours before opening full volume.

## Make the Switch Clean

The mechanics of migrating from Brevo to Smartlead are straightforward: export, authenticate, warm, rebuild, verify. The failure modes are almost always in the sequencing of those steps, not the steps themselves. Suppressions before lists. Warmup before sending. Test before full volume. Follow that order and you arrive at Smartlead with a healthier sending setup than you had on Brevo.
