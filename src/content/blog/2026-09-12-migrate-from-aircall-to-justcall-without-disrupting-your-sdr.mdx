## Before you migrate

Do not touch your JustCall account until you have completed the prep checklist below. Skipping even one item is how teams end up re-entering two years of call history by hand. I have seen it happen. It is painful, it is preventable, and there is no excuse for it.

- **Export your Aircall call logs.** Go to Analytics > Call Details, set the date range to your full account history, and export as CSV. Do this for every team workspace. Aircall caps exports at 10,000 rows per pull, so segment by quarter if your volume is high.
- **Download call recordings.** There is no bulk API. Use the Aircall API endpoint `GET /v1/calls` with a Zapier loop (or a simple n8n HTTP node) to hit each recording URL and pipe files to S3 or Google Drive. My preferred setup: a [Zapier](/go/zapier/) multi-step zap that triggers on each call record row, downloads the audio URL, uploads to Drive, and logs the Drive link to a tracking sheet.
- **Document your CRM integration mappings.** Screenshot or export every field mapping in Aircall's HubSpot or Salesforce connector. JustCall maps fields differently by default and you will lose your custom disposition-to-deal-stage logic if you do not write it down first.
- **List every active number.** Export your number inventory from Aircall's Numbers settings page. Flag which numbers are on live campaigns, which are sitting in email signatures, and which handle inbound routing.
- **Pause or notify active sequences.** If you are running outbound sequences in Outreach, Salesloft, or a similar tool that dials through Aircall, pause those steps now. Re-activate them only after JustCall is verified live.
- **Confirm your Aircall contract end date.** You need at least 10 business days of overlap before renewal to port numbers without a gap. If you are within 10 days of renewal, request a 30-day extension before initiating the port.

## What breaks and what does not transfer

Most of the migration is mechanical and predictable. The things that actually bite teams are subtle. Do not skip this section just because the steps above felt manageable.

    **Gotcha: Aircall call tags do not map 1:1 to JustCall outcome tags.**

    Aircall lets reps freehand call tags ("left VM," "callback Thu," "not interested"). JustCall uses a structured outcome tag system with a fixed list you configure. Any custom freehand tags from Aircall will not import. If your CRM workflows or reports depend on specific tag strings, those automations will silently stop firing after cutover. I have seen this break an entire set of HubSpot lifecycle-stage workflows at a client without anyone noticing for a full week.

    Also worth flagging: Aircall's "team performance" dashboard metrics do not export. Screenshot anything you use as a benchmark before you lose access.

**What transfers cleanly:** Your phone numbers (via porting), your team user list (re-invite by email), your CRM contact data (it lives in your CRM, not Aircall), and your SDR muscle memory. The JustCall dialer UI is familiar enough that ramp time is under a day for most reps.

**What does not transfer:** Call recordings (manual download required), call tag history, dashboard benchmarks, and any Aircall-specific Zapier or webhook automations. Rebuild those against JustCall's webhook schema, which is well-documented in the [JustCall developer docs](https://developers.justcall.io/).

## Verify it worked

Do not call the migration done until you have confirmed all four of these. Seriously. I have watched teams declare victory and then discover a logging gap three weeks later when pipeline reporting looked wrong.

1. **Make a test call from JustCall and confirm it logs to your CRM** with the correct contact association, call duration, recording link, and outcome tag.
2. **Send a test SMS from JustCall** and confirm it threads in both the JustCall inbox and the CRM contact timeline.
3. **Trigger a voicemail drop** on a test contact and confirm the audio plays correctly and the outcome tag fires.
4. **Pull a one-week activity report in your CRM** after cutover and verify call volume and outcome distribution match your pre-migration baseline. If the numbers look dramatically different, you have a logging gap, not a performance problem.

If you have [HubSpot automations](/blog/2026-09-09-hubspot-automations-costing-you-deals-and-you-dont-know-it/) that branch on call dispositions, specifically check those workflow enrollment triggers. They are the most common silent failure mode after a dialer switch. According to [HubSpot's integration documentation](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/workflows/create-workflows), workflow re-enrollment criteria do not automatically update when the underlying property source changes, which is exactly what happens when you swap dialers.

An Aircall-to-JustCall migration is one of the highest-ROI infrastructure swaps a RevOps team can run this year.
