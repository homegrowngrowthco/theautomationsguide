If your team is hunting for [Constant Contact](/go/constant-contact/) alternatives in 2026, you are probably running from one of two problems: the automation is too shallow to support a real nurture track, or the price stopped making sense once you crossed 5,000 contacts. ## How to choose

The first thing I'd check is whether your use case is primarily marketing email (newsletters, nurture, broadcast) or a blend of marketing and transactional. Sending product notifications, order confirmations, or triggered lifecycle emails alongside your campaign sends? Brevo's volume-based pricing and unified transactional layer make it the structurally smarter choice. You avoid managing two platforms and two billing relationships.

If your GTM motion runs on content, think webinars, gated assets, and lead scoring tied to content consumption, GetResponse is the stronger pick. Its automation builder lets you wire content engagement directly into your nurture logic without routing everything through [Zapier](/go/zapier/) or [n8n](/tools/n8n/). The webinar tool is genuinely native, not a Zoom embed, which keeps the data loop cleaner.

Constant Contact drops out of the B2B consideration set quickly once you need any of this. Its strengths are fast setup and event management. If neither is your bottleneck, there is no compelling reason to pay Constant Contact's contact-count-based pricing when Brevo's free tier or GetResponse's entry plan gives you materially more capability.

Teams shopping for [ActiveCampaign alternatives](/blog/2026-10-14-woodpecker-pricing-in-2026-plans-limits-and-when-to-switch/) should also be honest about whether the problem is price or features. Price? Brevo or GetResponse solve it cleanly. Features, meaning you need deeper CRM logic rather than cheaper sending? ActiveCampaign is still worth the budget. The alternatives will disappoint you faster than the billing will.

## The bottom line

For small B2B teams evaluating Constant Contact alternatives in 2026, the decision comes down to one variable: how much automation sophistication do you actually need? Brevo is the pragmatic pick if your primary constraint is cost and you want a single platform for both marketing and transactional sends. [GetResponse](/tools/getresponse/) is the better pick if your sequences need conditional logic, lead scoring, or webinar-tied triggers. Constant Contact earns a spot only if fast setup and event management matter more than depth. And if none of the three gets you where you need to be, GetResponse and [Brevo](/tools/brevo/) both serve as honest stepping stones before you move up to ActiveCampaign.
