## How to choose the right Outreach replacement

The decision almost always comes down to two questions: where does your source-of-truth CRM live, and what is the real sending volume per rep per month.

If your team is Salesforce-native and averaging more than 400 touchpoints per rep per month, [Apollo](/go/apollo) is the only one of these three that handles the volume and syncs back to Salesforce without a brittle Zapier layer holding things together. I have watched HubSpot Sequences stall out when reps hit enrollment limits and start creating duplicate contacts to work around them. That is not a training problem. That is a tool mismatch.

If you have already consolidated your GTM stack on [HubSpot](/go/hubspot), adding Sales Hub Professional to get sequences is a much easier internal sell than standing up a second vendor. The ops overhead is lower, reporting is cleaner because deal data and engagement data share the same schema, and your enablement team trains reps on one interface instead of two. The caveat: [HubSpot's documentation on sequences](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/sequences/create-and-edit-sequences) makes clear that sequences only work for contacts with an assigned owner and a tracked email. High-volume outbound to cold lists will hit friction fast.

<MyTake>
Every migration audit I have run turns up the same finding: reps are using sequences, call logging, and maybe two reports. Full stop. The governance layer, AI coaching, and Kaia call intelligence that justify Outreach's premium are almost always items that RevOps put on the roadmap and never finished configuring. Do the usage audit before you spec a replacement. Otherwise you are paying for unused features in a cheaper wrapper, which is not actually a win.
</MyTake>

## Make the switch without burning your pipeline

Before you cancel the Outreach contract, export your sequence performance data, open rates, reply rates, step-level conversion, and use it to prioritize which sequences to rebuild first. The [Outreach platform documentation on data exports](https://support.outreach.io/hc/en-us/articles/360060117094) covers the CSV format for sequence and prospect data. Rebuild your top five sequences by reply rate. Get three reps running them in the new platform. Validate deliverability before you cut over the full team.

That 4-to-6 week window is not pessimism. It is the minimum needed to avoid a pipeline gap. According to [Salesforce's State of Sales report](https://www.salesforce.com/resources/research-reports/state-of-sales/), rep productivity drops an average of 12 percent during tool transitions. A phased cutover is worth every extra week of dual-running costs.

The alternatives have genuinely matured. My clients who have made clean migrations to [Apollo](/go/apollo) or [HubSpot](/go/hubspot) Sequences are not missing Outreach. What they are missing is the six months they spent rationalizing the renewal before pulling the trigger.
