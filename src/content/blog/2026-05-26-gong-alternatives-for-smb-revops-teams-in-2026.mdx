## The Budget-Tier Framework

Before I drop the comparison table, let me give you the mental model I use with clients at [Homegrown Growth Co.](https://homegrowngrowth.co). Conversation intelligence tools in 2026 fall into three tiers based on what you actually get for your money, and most SMB teams are shopping the wrong tier entirely.

**Tier 1 (Free to $20/seat/month):** Recording, transcription, AI summaries, basic CRM push. This is Fathom and Fireflies territory. You get 80 percent of the daily utility Gong delivers. Reps stop taking notes, managers get call summaries in Slack, and HubSpot contact records auto-populate. The gap is analytics: you cannot slice win rates by talk track or flag at-risk deals by keyword trends across your whole pipeline.

**Tier 2 ($40 to $100/seat/month):** Add deal intelligence, coaching scorecards, and pipeline visibility. Avoma lives here. Honestly, this is where most SMB RevOps teams should land. You start getting signal at the pipeline level, not just the individual call level. I have run Avoma for two clients in the 15 to 30 rep range, and the manager visibility alone paid for itself in the first quarter. Stop second-guessing it and go straight to the Plus plan.

**Tier 3 ($100+/seat/month, often opaque pricing):** Gong, Salesloft Conversations, Outreach Kaia. Full revenue intelligence, deal risk scoring, forecast influence, deep integrations into your CRM's forecasting layer. Genuinely powerful. Also genuinely priced for teams where a RevOps analyst spends their whole week inside the platform. If that is not you, stop shopping here. According to [G2's 2024 Sales Technology Report](https://www.g2.com/categories/conversation-intelligence), most sub-50-rep teams never extract more than 30 percent of the features they pay for at this tier.

## How to Choose

The single most important question is not which tool has the best AI summaries. They are all good enough in 2026. The question is: where does this tool need to write data, and does it write data there reliably? I have watched Fireflies deployments fall apart not because the transcripts were bad but because the [HubSpot](https://go/hubspot) activity log was getting duplicate entries on every call. That burns rep trust faster than any UX problem ever will.

My decision rule for clients: if your team is under 8 reps and your primary need is "stop losing action items after calls," start with Fathom for free and upgrade later. If you are 8 to 30 reps with an active manager who wants to coach and inspect pipeline, go straight to Avoma's Plus plan and save yourself the migration pain of outgrowing Fathom in six months. Only look at Chorus if ZoomInfo is already on your contract renewal. And even then, run the math carefully.
