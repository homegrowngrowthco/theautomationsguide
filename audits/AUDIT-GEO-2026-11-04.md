## What this does and does not prove

**It shows:** in this sample the count did not move. No engine linked the domain in the pasted text, and the 13 cells with readable sources are a real "not cited".

**It does not show:**

- **Stability or trend.** This is one sample per cell, and engine answers vary by run. 1 then 1 does not show the ChatGPT hit is stable, or that PR #302 had no effect.
- **That the 11 unreadable cells are true misses.** A citation could sit behind a "+N" chip or a source list that did not paste.
- **That the chip is this site.** It is a name with no URL. It sits on the query the site was cited for at baseline, which makes that likely, but the text alone cannot confirm the domain.
- **That the run was clean.** The how-to-run step asks for a private window or fresh chat, but the sheet does not record which was used, and its date line is blank. The date used here is the day the sheet was saved.
